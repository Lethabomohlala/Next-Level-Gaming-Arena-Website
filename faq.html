FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Find quick answers to common questions about bookings, equipment, and house rules.

What are your operating hours?

We are open Monday through Thursday from 10:00 AM to 10:00 PM, and Friday through Sunday from 10:00 AM until midnight.

Do I need to make a reservation in advance?

While walk-ins are welcome based on availability, we strongly recommend booking online in advance—especially for VR stations, Esports coaching, and Escape Rooms.

How do the multi-pass discounts work?

When you book 2 packages/experiences in one transaction, you automatically receive 5% off. Book 3 for a 10% discount, or 4+ for a 15% discount on your subtotal!

Can I bring my own gear (peripherals/controller)?

Yes! You are welcome to bring your own mouse, keyboard, headset, or controller. All machines have accessible plug-and-play USB ports.

Is there an age limit for the arena or Escape Rooms?

Gamers of all ages are welcome! However, players under the age of 12 must be accompanied by an adult or legal guardian.
